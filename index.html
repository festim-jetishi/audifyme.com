Chapter V · The Author
memo · from the founder · apr 2026 commit 4a2b9c

Dear reader,

My grandfather was the reason I fell in love with books. He read to me before I could read myself, and the stories always felt bigger in his voice than they ever did on the page.

I built Audifyme for the people who already love books — and just as much for the people who don't think they do. A lot of people say they "aren't readers". Usually what they mean is they never found the right voice to read to them. A story hits differently when it's narrated by someone whose voice you actually want in your head — a friend, a parent, a character you made up from scratch. That's the part I wanted to unlock.

If you already love books, Audifyme is a new way to experience them. A familiar novel in a completely different voice becomes a completely different book. And if you've never finished one, that's fine too — you don't have to read. You can just listen. Anywhere. In any voice you want.

My hope is simple: more stories, reaching more people, in more voices than publishers could ever print.

Thanks for being here.

- Festim Jetishi, Switzerland

Why three tiers and no "buy credits"?

Audiobooks aren't a vending machine. You're subscribing to a story, not a unit of audio. We only generate narration in the moments you actually press play — so if you pay $5.99 and listen to two hours out of twenty, we simply eat the savings. That's how the prices stay honest.

Chapter VIII · Questions

Questions you're about to ask.

new Can I start a book with one narrator and switch mid-chapter?+
Yes — and it's the feature nobody else has. Start Dune with a pro narrator, decide 40 minutes in that it'd hit harder in your brother's voice, tap the narrator name, pick him. The book re-narrates from where you are. Your place, your bookmarks, your highlights — all preserved. Swap as many times as you want. No re-downloads, no weird gap in the audio.
Can I, uh... describe a very specific voice when making one?+
The synthesis engine takes a text description and invents a voice that matches. What you describe is up to you. We don't police adjectives. We'd just politely ask you not to share the output publicly, and not to use it for anything commercial. What happens in your headphones at 11pm while you're being read Crime & Punishment is between you and Dostoyevsky.
Can I reimagine a book with a totally different narrator?+
Yes. That's half the fun. A familiar book sounds wildly different when you swap in your best friend, your dad, or a voice you designed from scratch — and because you can change the narrator mid-book, committing costs you nothing.
What happens if I upload a PDF with pictures?+
We extract the text. OCR for scanned PDFs is coming later this year.
What file types can I bring in?+
PDF, EPUB, plain text, and the weird one-off document your friend swears is readable. If the text can be extracted cleanly, Audifyme can usually turn it into a book.
How is it so cheap?+
We only generate audio when you're actually listening. Most people don't finish audiobooks. Most subscription services bill you for the full thing anyway. We don't.
Can I download an audiobook to listen offline?+
Yes - per chapter. We hid "download the whole book" deep in Settings on purpose. It's more expensive for us, and it's also how you end up with 4GB of audio you'll never listen to.
Why iOS only?+
Because I had to start somewhere and I have an iPhone. Android is in the works. Web version later.
Who's behind Audifyme?+
Me. Festim Jetishi. One person, Switzerland-based, two cats. That's the team. Email me.
What if I hate it?+
Cancel in one tap. Apple/Google handle refunds. I'd love to hear why though - jetishi.festim@gmail.com.
Is there a referral program?+
Not yet. If you tell a friend and it gets them back into books, that's enough marketing for me.
